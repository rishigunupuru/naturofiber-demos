Natural-fibre & hybrid FRP composites

Structural composites, engineered from natural fibre.

NaturoFiber develops natural-fibre and hybrid fibre-reinforced polymer (FRP) composites — building panels, rebar and structural profiles — that are lighter than steel, resist corrosion and carry a lower carbon footprint than conventional glass- and carbon-fibre composites.

01The problem

Composites built modern industry. They also built a carbon and waste problem.

Automotive, aerospace, rail, packaging and green building in India all depend on energy-intensive synthetic fibre-reinforced polymers such as glass and carbon fibre.

100,000+MT

Composite waste generated in India every year. End-of-life FRP is landfilled or incinerated.

Source: PRT Innovations Pvt Ltd

1.7–2.5t CO₂e

Emitted for every tonne of glass fibre produced, plus ~17 GJ of energy per tonne of molten glass.

Source: Fortune Business Insights

0%

Bio-based content in conventional fibreglass sheets, earning no local-material credit under IGBC and GRIHA.

Source: IGBC / GRIHA rating frameworks

02Our approach

Two routes to a lighter, lower-carbon composite.

Route 01

Natural-fibre composites

Locally sourced natural fibres such as kenaf replace synthetic glass fibre in non-structural and semi-structural components.

Route 02

Hybrid natural / synthetic composites

Natural-fibre plies are interlaminated with high-performance carbon or glass fibre, putting strength exactly where the load path needs it — for demanding structural applications.

Highlight

Functional gradation

Fibre type, orientation and placement are graded through the thickness, so high-performance material goes only where performance is required. That is where the cost and carbon saving comes from.

  • Renewable reinforcement
  • Engineered fibre architecture
  • Lightweight construction
  • Hybrid performance
  • Scalable manufacturing
  • Application-specific design

04Why NaturoFiber

How it compares.

Comparison of conventional steel, conventional FRP and NaturoFiber composites
Parameter Conventional steel Conventional FRP NaturoFiber
Reinforcement Steel / metallic Glass, carbon or aramid fibre Natural fibre + hybrid architectures
Corrosion Needs protection and maintenance Generally corrosion-resistant Corrosion-resistant polymer composite
Weight Heavy — handling and transport penalty Lightweight, high specific strength Lightweight hybrid elements, high specific strength
Carbon footprint Energy-intensive production Relies on energy-intensive synthetic fibre Renewable fibre content, LCA-benchmarked
Material tailoring Grade and section selection Fibre type, orientation and lay-up Plus hybridisation and functional gradation
Ductility Ductile, but corrosion-prone Can be brittle Hybrid lay-up and toughened matrix for better energy absorption
